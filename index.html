Hello, world

I'm Daniël, welcome to my developer portfolio!


Here I showcase a few projects I have worked on during- and next to my studies.


The studies I completed are "Software developer" at the Alfa-college and "Communication & Multimedia design" at the Hanzehogeschool.
At both these studies I was taught how to design interactive media experiences, by focusing on the needs and preferences of the target audience.


While my studies covered all aspects of multimedia design and team-based collaboration, I have gravitated the most towards the technical side of design.
I've specialized in the logic and architecture of applications and websites - translating concepts into a digital experience.
